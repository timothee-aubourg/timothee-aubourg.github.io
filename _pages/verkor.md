## Context

Verkor is a European battery cell manufacturer. I worked on the pilot production line, during the scale-up phase, when processes were still changing.

## The problem

Predicting battery cell performance from data generated by multiple physical machines along the pilot line. Two constraints shaped the work: the approach had to stay cheap and simple enough to scale beyond a pilot line, and the process itself kept changing while data accumulated against older configurations.

## What I built

Predictive ML pipelines for cell performance forecasting, and monitoring and drift detection frameworks to track how those models behaved against live production data.
