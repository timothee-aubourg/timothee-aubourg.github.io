## Context

Verkor is one of Europe's first large-scale battery gigafactories. I joined during the scale-up phase, when the pilot production line was running and processes were still changing week to week.

## The problem

Predicting battery cell performance from data generated by multiple physical machines along the pilot line. Two constraints shaped the work: the approach had to stay cheap and simple enough to scale from a pilot line to a gigafactory, and the process itself kept changing while data accumulated against the older configurations.

## What I built

Predictive ML pipelines for cell performance forecasting, and monitoring and drift detection frameworks to track how those models behaved once they were running against live production data.

## Stack

Python · Spark · Databricks · Streamlit · Docker · Git/GitHub
