Between 2021 and 2024 I worked as a data scientist and AI engineer in industry, on sensor data and machine learning systems deployed in production environments. I moved into industry for caring reasons that called for more stability at the time, and returned to academic research in 2024.

Both roles were about the same underlying problem: getting a model to work in a real environment is a different task from getting it to work on a dataset. The difference is engineering rigour, honest validation, and understanding who has to use the output.

## [Verkor: predictive ML for battery cell performance](/industry/verkor/)
**AI Innovation Senior Engineer, Grenoble (2023–2024)**

Predictive models for battery cell performance across a pilot production line at one of Europe's first large-scale gigafactories, with monitoring and drift detection for models running in production.
